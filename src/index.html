LOOK BEFORE YOU TRUST

A closer look.

Use the remote view to reveal a page’s instructions, then capture another report. Clipboard sharing and file transfer are disabled in the viewer. Never follow instructions in your own terminal or enter passwords.

What does the analysis look for?

Verification paired with Windows Run or terminal instructions, command-pasting prompts, clipboard-writing code, and verification downloads. Findings include captured evidence and uncertainty.

What happens to the session?

The browser automatically expires after one minute, including if you close this tab. No extensions. Reports stay in this tab and disappear on reload. Submitted origins and bounded activity events are retained for 30 days. URL paths, query strings, fragments, page content and video are not retained in activity logs. The navigation address is encrypted temporarily and cleared on stop or terminal startup failure. Session records become eligible for deletion after the session allowance plus 15 minutes; deletion is asynchronous. Rate-limit records expire automatically.

What can this miss?

This is a heuristic inspection, not a safety guarantee. Hidden scripts, frames, delayed behavior, and visitor targeting may be missed. It does not execute downloaded files, run shell commands, or solve CAPTCHAs automatically.
